# Video script (≈ 2 minutes) - optional, but it helps "Data Presentation"

The criterion also evaluates explaining findings "to a non-technical audience". Speak to camera or over a screen recording; avoid acronyms.

| Time | Screen | Suggested text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Summary (banner and title) | "In the NBA, coaches substitute a player when they see them tired. We wanted to know whether the data back that intuition." |
| 0:15–0:35 | "In 30 seconds" | "We analysed more than 870 thousand shots from four seasons. For each shot we computed how many consecutive minutes the player had been on court, and discounted how hard the shot was." |
| 0:35–1:00 | Chart by consecutive minutes and the Evidence tab ("Does it repeat every season?") | "Result: up to about 12 consecutive minutes, accuracy does not drop in a measurable way, and that repeats in each of the four seasons. Current rotations already avoid the problem." |
| 1:00–1:20 | Evidence → cold start | "What we do see is a cold start: in the first 30 seconds after entering, players score almost 2 points below their norm, and it normalises toward minute 2. It shows in all four seasons." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Placebo (bars 60% / 59%) | "A widely used rule, 'substitute if accuracy drops 3 points', flags 60% of players. But it also flags 59% when the minutes are random. That is noise." |
| 1:35–1:55 | 3D simulator (change rival and defender distance) | "And in the simulator, every part of the probability comes from data: the player, the shot distance, the rival team, the defender distance, the cold start. A Curry three goes from 37% with the defender far away to 22% with the defender on top of him." |
| 1:55–2:10 | New player / closing | "With these data a single player cannot be singled out as the one who tires, and the tool says so instead of inventing a number. Thank you." |

Tips: record at 1080p, visible cursor, calm pace. If it does not fit in 2 minutes, skip the New player segment. The simulator figure (37% → 22%) is a top-of-the-key Curry three with the defender at 8 ft and at 1.5 ft; check it on screen before recording.
